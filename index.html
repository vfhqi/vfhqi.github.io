For 30 minutes, every page on these sites comes only from the saved copy, so you can check what you will see without internet. It switches itself off; untick it to stop sooner.

How to use it

  • Before you go offline: open this page on Wi-Fi. It updates by itself when the saved copy is more than a day old; leave the page open until it says it is up to date.
  • When offline: open your usual bookmarks or the links above. Pages you have not saved show a short note with a link back here.
  • On the iPad: use ordinary Safari or Chrome tabs, not a Home Screen icon, and open this page at least once a week, or the iPad may clear the saved copy.
  • Research reports are not saved in bulk; any page you open online is kept for offline reading as well.
